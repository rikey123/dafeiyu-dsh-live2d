# Mandarin IPA mouth shapes

`mandarin-visemes.json` is a deliberately approximate mapping to this application's five available vowel controls, not a model of lip physiology. It is informed by the phoneme groupings in Hugging Face speech-to-speech `src/speech_to_speech/STV/phoneme_viseme_map.json` (p/b/m share a closed-mouth group; i/j and u/w share groups) and the actual MFA Mandarin greeting inventory. The upstream map targets a different set of visemes, so the five-shape reduction and rounded y/ɥ mapping are local approximations, not a verbatim upstream map. Tone marks and nasalization are removed; compound vowels use a static blended shape because the alignment does not provide internal boundaries. Unknown phones fall back to the live MFCC path. See the upstream repository's Apache-2.0 license for its map.
